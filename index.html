Common Questions

Straight
answers.

No fluff. Here's what most folks ask before booking.

What areas does BR Lawn Care serve? +

We serve residential and commercial properties across Eastern North Carolina — including Greenville, Rocky Mount, Wilson, Goldsboro, Kinston, Tarboro, Farmville, Washington, New Bern, Jacksonville, Havelock, Winterville, Ayden, Bethel, Grifton, Snow Hill, and Williamston. Counties covered include Pitt, Edgecombe, Nash, Wilson, Wayne, Lenoir, Beaufort, Craven, Martin, and Greene. Not sure if we cover your area? Call 252.503.0984 and we'll tell you straight.

How much does lawn care cost? +

Lawn care starts at $55. We offer one-time, weekly, and bi-weekly schedules. Every estimate is free and includes a straightforward price with no hidden fees or upsells.

Are you insured? +

Yes — BR Lawn Care is fully insured for residential and commercial work. We can provide a certificate of insurance on request for HOAs, property managers, and commercial clients.

Do you offer free estimates? +

Yes. Every estimate is free with no obligation. Request one at our contact page or call 252.503.0984 — we reply within 24 hours, usually same-day.

How fast do you reply to estimate requests? +

Within 24 hours, usually the same day. Final scheduling depends on the property, requested scope, route, and current availability.

What should I include in my estimate request? +

Property address or general area, the services you think you need (or just say "not sure"), approximate size of the area, your timeline, and any photos you can share. The more detail, the more accurate the quote.

What services do you offer beyond mowing? +

Tree and stump removal, land clearing, landscape installs (sod, mulch, rock, plantings), pressure washing, and ongoing property management for rentals, HOAs, and commercial lots.

Do you handle commercial and HOA accounts? +

Yes. We manage commercial lots, rental portfolios, and HOA common areas with consistent schedules, one point of contact, and one monthly invoice.

What are BR Lawn Care's hours? +

Monday through Saturday, 7:00 AM to 7:00 PM. Closed Sundays. For after-hours storm damage or urgent jobs, leave a message and we'll call back first thing.

What's the best way to contact BR Lawn Care? +

Call or text 252.503.0984 for the fastest response, or fill out the free estimate form at our contact page. Hours are Mon–Sat, 7am–7pm.
