Service FAQ

Tree & stump
questions.

Do you haul away all the debris after removal? +

Yes. Every tree removal job includes complete debris cleanup and haul-off from the property. Limbs, brush, and trunk sections are loaded and removed — you won't be left with a pile at the curb. If you'd like logs split and stacked for firewood, just let us know when you request the estimate.

How deep do you grind stumps? +

We grind stumps below grade — typically 6 to 12 inches depending on the stump size and your intended use of the area. After grinding, we fill the cavity with the resulting wood chips or topsoil so the surface is level and ready for grass, mulch, or new landscape work.

Are you insured for tree removal work? +

Yes. BR Lawn Care is fully insured for tree removal, including general liability and equipment coverage. A certificate of insurance is available on request — just ask when you reach out for your estimate. We never cut corners on safety or coverage.

Is a dead or leaning tree priced differently? +

Pricing is based on height, trunk diameter, accessibility, and degree of lean — not simply whether the tree is dead or alive. Dead trees can sometimes cost less to remove since the wood is lighter, but a severe lean toward a structure or tight access can increase the cost. Send photos and we'll give you an honest assessment.
