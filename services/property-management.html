Service FAQ

Property management
questions.

Do you service HOA communities? +

Yes. We work with HOAs throughout Eastern NC to maintain common areas, entranceways, detention ponds, and individual unit grounds as required by association standards. We can provide a scope of work and pricing for board review, and we carry full insurance with COI available on request for HOA records.

Can I get one invoice for multiple properties? +

Yes. Property managers and landlords with multiple units receive consolidated invoicing. We can break it out by property address for your records or roll it into a single line item — whatever works best for your accounting. Multi-property accounts also benefit from priority scheduling across all locations.

What information do I need to provide to get started? +

To get started we need: the property address(es), the scope of services you want covered (mowing, cleanups, pressure washing, etc.), your preferred service frequency, and any notes on access, gates, or tenant communication preferences. Photos of the property are helpful but not required. We'll build a custom proposal around your specifics.

Do you provide a certificate of insurance? +

Yes. BR Lawn Care is fully insured and a certificate of insurance is available upon request. For property managers, HOAs, and commercial clients who require proof of coverage before work begins, just ask when you reach out and we'll provide the COI before the first service.
