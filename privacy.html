Privacy notice

Last updated August 26, 2026. This notice explains the website information used when someone visits brlawncarenc.com or sends an estimate request.

Estimate requests

The contact form asks for details such as your name, phone number, email address, property area, selected services, and message so BR Lawn Care can review and respond to your request. Form submissions are transmitted through Formspree. Do not submit payment information, account credentials, medical information, or other sensitive records.

Optional website analytics

Google Analytics is managed through Google Tag Manager and remains off unless you choose “Allow analytics.” If allowed, Google may process standard page, browser, device, referral, and interaction data. BR Lawn Care’s analytics events do not include the values entered into the estimate form. Advertising storage and ad-personalization signals remain disabled.

Your choice

Your analytics choice is saved in your browser. Global Privacy Control and enabled Do Not Track signals are honored by keeping optional analytics disabled. You may clear your saved choice below and choose again.

After selecting this button, the privacy choice panel will appear on this page unless your browser sends a supported privacy signal.

Questions

For a question about an estimate request or this notice, call BR Lawn Care at 252.503.0984.
