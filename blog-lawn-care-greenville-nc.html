Greenville's lawn season runs longer than most people plan for

Greenville's climate means grass is actively growing from late March through early November. Bermuda and centipede — the two most common turf types in Pitt County — grow aggressively through the summer heat. That means weekly mowing from May through August, bi-weekly from March through April and September through October, and at least one fall cleanup before things go dormant.

If you've been on bi-weekly mowing and let it run three or four weeks between cuts, that first visit will cost more — a lot of extra material to remove and more time to get it back to spec. The cleanest and cheapest path is a consistent schedule from the start.

What a standard lawn care visit includes

  • Mowing to your specified height — typically 3 to 3.5 inches for Bermuda, 2.5 to 3 for centipede
  • Edging along every driveway, sidewalk, and walkway
  • String trimming around all obstacles — trees, beds, fences, AC units
  • Blowing off all hard surfaces before leaving
  • Bagging or mulching depending on volume and preference

What's not included by default: weed control, fertilizing, or irrigation maintenance. Those are separate conversations depending on what the property needs.

Pricing for Greenville lawn care

Residential mowing in Greenville starts at $55 for a standard lot. Most single-family homes in Pitt County run between $55 and $95 depending on lot size, terrain, and obstacles. Corner lots and properties with a lot of trimming around beds or fence lines typically land on the higher end.

We don't charge travel fees within our service area, and there are no hidden add-ons for blowing off the driveway or edging — that's standard on every visit.

What to send for a fast estimate

The fastest way to get an accurate quote is to send the property address, a note on the service (mowing, one-time, recurring), and a few photos of the yard. If the lot is large or has multiple zones, a rough size estimate helps. We typically reply within a few hours — sometimes the same morning.

Don't worry if you're not sure what you need. "Not sure, just needs mowing" is a completely fine starting point and we'll figure out the rest from the photos.
