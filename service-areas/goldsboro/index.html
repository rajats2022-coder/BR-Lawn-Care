Build the Goldsboro request

Build the request around the property’s next use.

Goldsboro customers can help BR understand urgency by explaining whether the property needs ongoing presentation, a turnover cleanup, or a defined outdoor improvement.

Move-in or turnover work

List the exterior items needed before occupancy and separate immediate cleanup from improvements that can be priced as options.

Bed or lawn refresh

Show worn areas, existing borders, material to remove, and whether the desired finish uses sod, mulch, rock, or planting.

Tree or stump concern

Include several angles, approximate size, nearby structures, access width, and the cleanup condition expected after the work.

What to include

Help the crew understand the property.

  • Property address and type
  • Photos of the work area
  • Desired result and timing
  • Gates, tenants, pets, utilities, or access limits
